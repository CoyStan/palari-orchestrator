# SEC-0001 Human Report

## Why This Mattered

The Company AI OS roadmap needs an honest way to see whether governance is
still lightweight/local or closer to a stronger posture before any future
autonomous authority is considered.

## What Changed

SEC-0001 adds:

- `palari doctor secure`
- `palari doctor governance`

The output reports ForgeGate, broker side effects, broker observations, policy
acceptance, branch-protection verification limits, and R5 human approval
configuration.

## What I Should Know

The doctor is read-only. It does not enable ForgeGate, activate policies, grant
broker side effects, verify hosted branch protection, or change ticket
acceptance.

## What To Check

- Weak posture is clear when ForgeGate is disabled or broker observations are
  absent.
- Stronger local posture does not imply hosted branch protection.
- R5 human approval remains explicit.
