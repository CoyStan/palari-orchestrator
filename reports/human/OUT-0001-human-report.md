# OUT-0001 Human Report

## Why This Mattered

Palari needs a way to remember what happened after governed work so future
policy and Human Governance Load improvements are based on observed outcomes,
not vibes.

## What Changed

OUT-0001 adds:

- `palari outcome create`
- `palari outcome list`
- `palari outcome show`
- `palari outcome lint`
- `palari outcome record`

Policy candidates can also cite recorded outcomes linked to their source
tickets or decisions.

## What I Should Know

Outcomes do not accept work, activate policies, or prove business impact unless
evidence is linked. They are ledger records for review and future learning.

## What To Check

- Outcome records link to real workflows, tickets, decisions, goals, and
  evidence where relevant.
- Pending or invalidated outcomes are not treated as success proof.
- Policy candidates use outcomes as context only.
